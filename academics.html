  • Arabic

  • Geometry
  • Sciences
  • History
  • Geography

03Leaves · Grade 4 – 7

Upper Primary

Knowledge grows leaf by leaf. The CBSE curriculum builds on the Montessori foundation, with labs, a library and counselling support.

Beyond the classroom

Programmes that grow with families

SEED Smile

A common platform for SEED Montessori students and Montessori training aspirants: a five/six-year integrated programme for children and a one-year training programme for aspiring teachers.

SEED Aspire

Parents as partners: regular orientation sessions, motivational programmes for students, teachers and parents, and frequent parent–teacher communication.

MTTC

Montessori Teacher Training

The Montessori Teachers Training Course, offered under KELTRON, a Government of Kerala undertaking, for those who want to become Montessori educators.
