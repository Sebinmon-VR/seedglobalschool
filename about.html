Principal's message

“SEED Global School has a strong tradition of holistic and multidisciplinary education, aiming to develop all capacities of human beings: intellectual, aesthetic, social, physical, emotional and moral.”

Through experiential learning, dedicated faculty and a rich curriculum, our students learn how to learn: thinking critically, solving problems, creating and collaborating. We believe every challenge is an opportunity for growth, and we invite every parent to be our partner in that journey.

The trust

Global Educational Trust

Established at Maranchery in 2017, the trust aims to build and develop schools, colleges, training institutes, computer education centres, employment training centres, counselling centres and a sports academy.

It also runs awareness programmes on health, education, women's empowerment, humanism, and religious and communal harmony.
